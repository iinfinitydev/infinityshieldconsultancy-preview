Service 01

Fire Risk Assessments & Fire Safety Audits

Comprehensive reviews of existing buildings to identify fire and life safety risks, compliance gaps and practical corrective actions.

Reports are written so owners, managers and design teams can act, and so the record stands up to later scrutiny by authorities, insurers and boards.

Scope

What this service covers

  • Fire Risk Assessments
  • Building fire safety audits
  • Occupancy and life safety reviews
  • Means of egress assessments
  • Emergency escape and evacuation reviews
  • Fire compartmentation assessment
  • Fire protection system observations
  • Fire safety management reviews
  • Prioritised corrective action plans
  • Reinspection and close out verification
