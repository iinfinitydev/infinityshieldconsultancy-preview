Service 05

External Wall, Façade & Cladding Fire Safety

Specialist assessment of complete external wall assemblies — materials, insulation, cavities, barriers and perimeter fire-stopping.

Specialist assessment of complete external wall assemblies, including façade materials, insulation, cavities, barriers, interfaces and perimeter fire-stopping.

UK experience informs how we work. UAE assessments are based on the applicable UAE and local-authority framework — this is not marketed as an EWS1 service.

What this service covers

  • External wall fire-safety assessments
  • Façade inspections
  • Cladding assessments
  • ACP assessments
  • Insulation assessment
  • Cavity barrier inspections
  • Curtain-wall fire-stopping
  • Slab-edge / perimeter fire barriers
  • Façade system certification and evidence review
  • Intrusive inspections and targeted opening-up
  • Applicable fire-test evidence review
  • Remediation strategies
  • Remediation monitoring
  • Completion verification

External wall services are not offered as a UAE EWS1 product. Assessments follow the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice and the requirements of the relevant Authority Having Jurisdiction.
