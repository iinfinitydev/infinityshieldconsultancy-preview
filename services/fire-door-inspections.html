Service 03

Fire Door Inspection & Consultancy

Detailed inspection of fire-resisting doorsets — components, installation condition and ongoing performance.

Detailed inspection and assessment of fire-resisting doorsets, their components, installation condition and ongoing performance.

Asset registers and photographic defect schedules so the estate can be managed, not just reported once.

What this service covers

  • Fire door condition surveys
  • Fire door compliance inspections
  • Door leaf and frame assessment
  • Ironmongery assessment
  • Self-closing device checks
  • Intumescent and smoke-seal inspection
  • Glazing assessment
  • Threshold and gap assessment
  • Fire-door identification and asset registers
  • Photographic defect schedules
  • Repair-versus-replacement recommendations
  • Remediation specifications
  • Post-remediation verification
