The consultancy model

Inspect. Identify. Assess. Specify. Monitor. Verify. Close out.

A simple explanation of how we work. The same sequence supports a single fire door or a remediation programme across several buildings.

01

Inspect

Review the building, drawings and fire critical details before they are concealed.

02

Identify

Record defects, compliance gaps and life safety risks with evidence.

03

Assess

Judge technical significance, priority and the applicable UAE framework.

04

Specify

Set the remediation scope, specification and acceptance criteria.

05

Monitor

Check workmanship while fire critical works are still open.

06

Verify

Confirm completed works against the specification and the evidence.

07

Close out

Issue records, defect close out and handover documentation.

Questions

Before you write.

Do you issue Civil Defence approvals?

No. We provide Civil Defence compliance consultancy, technical support for submissions and approval process support where it sits within our authorised scope. Approvals remain with the relevant authority.

Is façade work offered as EWS1?

No. External wall and cladding assessments in the UAE follow the applicable UAE and local authority framework. UK experience informs our method; it is not sold as a UAE EWS1 service.

Do you install, or only advise?

This company is the consultancy. Installation of passive fire protection and fire doors is delivered by Infinity Shield Technical Services LLC. Many clients use both, under one group, so strategy and workmanship stay aligned.

Which codes do you work to?

The UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice, applicable Civil Defence requirements, and the requirements of the relevant Authority Having Jurisdiction. NFPA and BS/EN are referenced where the project requires it.

How quickly do you respond?

We typically respond within four hours on UAE business days.
